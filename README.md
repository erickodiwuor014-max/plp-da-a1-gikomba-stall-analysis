## Business Problem

Wanjiku runs a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market. For the last two weeks she has recorded her daily sales in a notebook and kept a rough record of her stock, but she does not know how the business is really doing. She wants to know whether sales are growing, which days matter most, which products she needs to restock, and which products earn her the best margin. Using only core Python (no pandas or NumPy), this project turns her 14 days of sales and her stock list into clear numbers and practical recommendations.

## Top Three Findings

1. **Sales are growing:** weekly sales rose **10.66%**, from KES 22,050 in week 1 to KES 24,400 in week 2 (total KES 46,450; average KES 3,317.86 a day).
2. **Weekends are the money days:** Saturday and Sunday average **KES 4,900** a day versus **KES 2,685** on weekdays, which is **82.5% higher**. All 4 Strong days were weekend days, and all 6 Weak days were Mon, Tue or Thu.
3. **Two products need restocking now:** **Tomatoes** (18 kg vs a 25 kg reorder level) and **Sukuma** (9 kg vs 15 kg). Avocado has the best gross margin at **42.9%**.
